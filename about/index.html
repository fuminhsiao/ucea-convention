ABOUT

We invite you to join us at the 39th Annual UCEA Convention that will be held November 19–22, 2025, at the Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort and Casino in San Juan, Puerto Rico.

The 2025 Convention Planning Committee includes Shelby Cosner (University of Georgia), Kaneshia Dorsan (University of Georgia), Osly Flores (University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign), Hans Klar (Clemson University), Patricia Virella (Montclair State), and Karl Gildner (UCEA Events Manager).
